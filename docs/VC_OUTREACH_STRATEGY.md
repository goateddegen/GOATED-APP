## OUTREACH STRATEGY

### Strategy 1: Warm Introductions (Highest Conversion Rate)

Warm intros convert at 10-30x the rate of cold outreach. Every possible effort should be made to find a mutual connection before sending a cold email.

**How to generate warm intros:**

1. **Map your network.** List every person you know who might know a target investor. Second-degree connections on LinkedIn are the starting point.
2. **Portfolio company founders.** The fastest path to a VC is through their portfolio companies. If you know anyone at a16z, Sequoia, or Multicoin portfolio company, ask for an intro.
3. **Other founders.** Founders who have raised from your target VCs are the best intro source. They have active relationships and their word carries weight.
4. **Accelerator alumni.** YC, Techstars, and other alumni networks are goldmines for warm intros.
5. **Angel investors as bridges.** Angels who invest early often have relationships with institutional VCs. A $25K angel check from the right person can open doors to $500K institutional checks.

**Intro request template:**

```
Subject: Quick intro request -- [Investor Name] at [Firm]

Hey [Connector Name],

I'm building GOATED, an AI-native revenue intelligence platform -- think Apollo + Outreach +
market intelligence, but free. We earn 10% commission on closed deals instead of charging
subscriptions. Platform is live with real-time data from CoinGecko, Polymarket, and more.

I noticed you're connected to [Investor Name] at [Firm]. Their thesis around
[specific thesis -- e.g., "AI-first vertical SaaS"] aligns perfectly with what we're building.

Would you be open to making a quick intro? Happy to send a one-pager or deck that you can
forward along.

Appreciate it either way.

Best,
Tarek
```

### Strategy 2: Cold Email (Direct Outreach)

For investors where no warm intro path exists, cold email is the fallback. The key is radical personalization -- every email must reference the specific investor's thesis, portfolio, or public statements.

**Cold email template (personalize heavily per recipient):**

```
Subject: Free Apollo alternative -- 10% commission model (live platform)

Hi [First Name],

I read your [specific piece -- blog post / tweet / podcast appearance] about
[specific topic -- e.g., "commission-based SaaS replacing subscriptions"].
That thesis is exactly what I'm building.

GOATED is an AI-native revenue intelligence platform. It replaces Apollo,
Outreach, and market intelligence tools -- all free. We earn 10% commission
on deals closed through the platform.

What's live today:
- 500K+ AI-scored leads
- Real-time market intelligence (CoinGecko, Polymarket, sentiment analysis)
- AI co-pilot powered by Claude (Anthropic)
- VoIP/SMS outreach suite
- Solana wallet integration for crypto-native payments

Platform: [URL]

I'm raising a $500K pre-seed. Would a 15-minute call make sense to share
what I'm seeing in the data?

Best,
Tarek Ali
Founder, GOATED Solutions
```

**Cold email rules:**
- Send Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM in the investor's timezone
- Follow up exactly once, 5-7 days later (short, 2-sentence follow-up)
- Never follow up more than twice on a cold thread
- Personalization must reference something specific and recent (not "I admire your portfolio")
- Include the live platform URL -- showing a working product is your strongest signal

### Strategy 4: Twitter/X Presence Building

Crypto VCs and many tech investors are more active on Twitter/X than email. Building a presence here creates inbound investor interest.

**Content strategy:**

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Build-in-public updates | 3-5x/week | Show velocity, transparency, attract attention |
| Product demos (screen recordings) | 1x/week | Visual proof of working product |
| Market analysis threads | 1x/week | Demonstrate domain expertise |
| Contrarian takes on sales tools | 2x/week | Generate engagement, show vision |

**Engagement strategy:**
- Follow and engage with target investors' tweets (genuine, thoughtful replies -- not sycophantic)
- Quote-tweet with added analysis when investors post about relevant topics
- Tag investors when sharing relevant data or insights (sparingly -- 1-2x/month max)
- Participate in Twitter Spaces hosted by target VCs or crypto communities

**Target followers:** 5,000+ within 3 months (achievable with consistent, high-quality content in the sales/AI/crypto niche)

### Strategy 5: "Build in Public" as an Investor Funnel

The "build in public" movement has produced multiple successful fundraises where investors reached out to founders, not the other way around.

**Build-in-public playbook:**

1. **Weekly updates.** Every Friday, post a thread or blog post with: what shipped, what's next, key metrics, biggest challenge, specific ask.
2. **Revenue transparency.** Share MRR growth publicly (when it starts). Nothing attracts investors like a visible revenue curve.
3. **Product development diary.** Share the "why" behind product decisions. Investors want to see strategic thinking, not just code output.
4. **Customer stories.** When users close deals using GOATED, share those stories (with permission). Nothing validates a model like real customer success.
5. **Honest struggle content.** Share the hard parts -- the bugs, the rejections, the pivots. Authenticity builds trust and audience.

**Platforms:** Twitter/X (primary), LinkedIn (secondary), personal blog (long-form)

## PITCH DECK FORMAT (In-Person / Zoom Meetings)

When an investor agrees to a meeting, use the following 12-slide structure. The entire deck should take 10-12 minutes to present, leaving 18-20 minutes for Q&A.

### Slide Structure

| Slide | Content | Time |
|-------|---------|------|
| 1. Title | GOATED Solutions -- Revenue Intelligence OS. Logo, tagline, founder name. | 15 sec |
| 2. Problem | Sales teams use 5-8 tools, $500-$2K/seat/month. SMBs priced out. Siloed data. AI bolted on. | 90 sec |
| 3. Solution | All-in-one AI-native platform. Free to use. 10% commission on closed deals. | 60 sec |
| 4. Demo | Live platform walkthrough: lead search, AI co-pilot, market intelligence, outreach. | 3 min |
| 5. Market Size | TAM $50B+. SAM $8B. SOM $200M. Market data sources cited. | 60 sec |
| 6. Business Model | Commission + enterprise + affiliate + data licensing. Unit economics. | 90 sec |
| 7. Traction | Platform live. Data integrations. Current metrics. User testimonials. | 60 sec |
| 8. Competition | Landscape matrix. Why "free + AI-native" wins. Unfair advantages. | 60 sec |
| 9. Go-to-Market | PLG flywheel. Crypto ICP first. Content + community + affiliate growth. | 60 sec |
| 10. Team | Founder background. Planned hires. Advisory board (if applicable). | 45 sec |
| 11. Financials | 18-month projection. Path to $100K MRR. Use of funds breakdown. | 60 sec |
| 12. The Ask | $500K pre-seed. SAFE at $3-5M post-money. Key milestones. | 30 sec |

### Presentation Rules

- **Lead with the demo.** A working product is the single strongest signal at pre-seed. Show the platform within the first 3 minutes.
- **Know the numbers cold.** TAM, SAM, SOM, unit economics, CAC, LTV, MRR projections. Investors will probe on every number.
- **Anticipate objections.** Have clear, honest answers for: "Can a solo founder scale this?", "How do you prevent deal underreporting?", "Why won't Apollo launch a free tier?"
- **Show conviction without arrogance.** Investors bet on founders who are ambitious yet self-aware. Acknowledge risks. Show you have thought deeply about failure modes.
- **End with urgency.** "We are raising $500K. We have [X] committed. The round closes [date]." Create scarcity.

## INVESTOR FAQ (Prepared Responses)

### "Why will users pay 10% commission when they can use free tools separately?"

Because there are no free tools that do what GOATED does. You can use free LinkedIn for prospecting, but you cannot get AI-scored leads, integrated VoIP outreach, real-time market intelligence, and an AI co-pilot in any free product. And 10% of a deal you closed is far cheaper than $300-$2,000/month in tool subscriptions for deals you might not close.

### "How do you prevent users from finding leads on GOATED and closing deals off-platform?"

Three mechanisms: (1) The outreach suite is built into the platform -- calling, emailing, and texting happen within GOATED, creating a natural audit trail. (2) Pipeline tracking creates visibility into deal progress. (3) The value of the platform increases with usage -- AI gets better, data gets richer, outreach history compounds. Users who try to avoid commission lose access to these compounding benefits.

### "Can a solo founder build this?"

I am building it, and the platform is live. AI-augmented development has fundamentally changed what a solo founder can accomplish. That said, the $500K raise specifically funds hiring 2 senior engineers by month 3, reducing bus-factor risk and accelerating development velocity by 3-4x.

### "What if Apollo launches a free tier?"

Apollo's revenue is $100M+ ARR from subscriptions. Launching a free tier would cannibalize their core business. It would take a board-level decision and 12-18 months to execute. By then, GOATED will have network effects, community, and product differentiation that a free Apollo tier cannot replicate. Also, Apollo is not AI-native and does not have market intelligence or outreach built in.

### "What is your unfair advantage?"

Incentive alignment. Every incumbent charges subscriptions -- they get paid whether their customer closes deals or not. GOATED only gets paid when users succeed. This creates a fundamentally different product development incentive: we are not building features to justify a subscription. We are building features that help users close more deals, because that is how we generate revenue.
