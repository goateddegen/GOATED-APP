**Iteration framework (Wed morning, 30 min):**

Pull your CRM sheet. Look at:

1. **Time-of-day connect rate** — when are people actually picking up? If mornings dominate, shift weight there.
2. **Vertical connect rate** — is HVAC outperforming dental? Concentrate on the winner.
3. **Opening conversion** — of connects, how many engaged past the first 30 sec? If <30%, the opening needs work.
4. **Booking conversion** — of engaged connects, how many booked? If <20%, the close-the-meeting phrase needs work.

**Then pick ONE thing to change on Wednesday's calls.** Don't change three things at once — you won't know what worked.

Common Wednesday adjustments:
- Swap LOST-REV opening for SOCIAL-PROOF (if you have a Tue close to reference)
- Drop "Got 30 seconds?" → "Got 60 seconds?" (sometimes 30 feels too quick to them)
- Lead with the metro instead of the company name ("I'm calling every HVAC in Dallas this week...")
- Increase or decrease pace based on speech-to-pickup feedback

## Daily energy management (autistic-operator edition)

You're 20, hungry, dialing solo. The nervous system is going to throttle you mid-day if you don't manage it. Specifically:

### What WILL drain you faster than expected
- **Sugar crashes** — no sugary breakfast, no afternoon Red Bull
- **Phone scrolling between calls** — tanks dopamine reserves
- **Bad call recovery** — if you bomb a call, dwell time amplifies the next 5
- **Too much coffee** — heart rate up, voice gets shaky
- **Music too loud during prep** — overstimulates before you need calm focus
- **Skipping movement** — sitting for 4hr straight kills your voice

### What WILL keep you on
- **Protein-heavy breakfast** (eggs, meat, cheese — no carbs first)
- **Walking 10 min between dial blocks** — resets nervous system
- **Cold water on face after a hard call** — physiological reset (works, not woo)
- **Standing while dialing** — voice projects 30% better
- **Big bottle of water at desk** — sip every dial
- **Single AirPod or wired headset** — frees both hands, hears better
- **No music during dials** — silence between calls lets your brain process the last one
- **20-min nap at 1 PM if needed** — better than coffee at 2 PM

### Mid-day collapse protocol (around dial 30)

Symptoms: voice flat, energy gone, "what's the point" thoughts.

Protocol:
1. Stand up, walk outside for 5 min. NO PHONE.
2. 10 push-ups (or any push-up variation you can do)
3. Cold water — splash face or chug 16oz
4. Read the closer's creed out loud
5. Back at desk. First dial back: lowest-stakes prospect on the list (not your hottest one).
6. Get one easy "no" to break the rust. Then keep going.

## What to do if you're not closing by Wednesday

Reality check time. If you're at 0/3 days with zero closes:

**Do NOT:**
- Lower the price
- Stop dialing
- Switch verticals mid-week
- Go on Twitter and complain
- Quit and go to LinkedIn looking for jobs

**DO:**
1. **Call a peer** — message someone closing in a similar space, ask for a 15-min review of your script (offer to pay or trade)
2. **Record a discovery call** — listen to it. Where did it die? Adjust THAT one thing.
3. **Re-pull your list** — are these prospects too small? Too tire-kickery? Are you calling owners or front desks?
4. **Test ONE new opening** Wednesday morning — not three
5. **Lower expectations to "1 close in week 2 instead of week 1"** — adjust mentally, keep dialing

**This is normal.** Most cold-outbound operators don't close in Week 1. They close in Week 2 or 3 once the script + list + cadence calibrate. Don't break the cadence.
