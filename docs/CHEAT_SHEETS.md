- ONE callback number, repeated twice (start + end)

- "Talk soon" not "Have a great day"

## CHEAT SHEET 6: DISCOVERY CALL AGENDA (15-30 min)

```
0-2 min   Confirm time, mute notifications, share screen
2-5 min   "Tell me about your business — what's working, what's broken?"
5-10 min  Run live audit on their website. Lead with their number.
10-15 min Pitch one tier. Match their pain to one module from UPSELL_CATALOG
15-20 min Handle 2-3 objections (max — don't over-handle)
20-25 min "I have time slot Wednesday or Friday for kickoff. Which?"
25-28 min Send Stripe link. Wait for ping.
28-30 min Confirm next steps. "Welcome to GOATED. Talk Wednesday."
```

If they don't close on this call:
- Don't push >30 min
- Schedule ONE follow-up call
- Send Loom recap that evening

## CHEAT SHEET 11: ENERGY-CRASH RECOVERY (mid-day)

When you hit dial 30 and feel dead:

```
1. STAND. Walk outside 5 min. NO PHONE.
2. 10 push-ups (or pump up the heart rate any way)
3. Cold water on face / chug 16oz
4. Read the closer's creed once (CLOSING_PLAYBOOK.md)
5. First dial back: lowest-stakes person on the list
6. Get one easy 'no' to break rust
7. Resume normal dialing
```

This takes 8-10 minutes. Worth every second. Do not skip when you feel dead.

## CHEAT SHEET 15: WHAT TO DO IF YOU GET A REFUND REQUEST

(You will get one eventually.)

```
1. DO NOT panic. DO NOT argue.
2. Reply within 1 hour: "I'm so sorry it didn't work. Refunding now."
3. Issue refund in Stripe immediately.
4. ASK: "Before you go — what specifically didn't work? I want to learn."
5. Listen to their answer. Don't defend.
6. Add their feedback to a "lessons" doc.
7. Refund hits their card in 5-10 business days.
8. They're more likely to refer you LATER if you handle this well.
```

Bad refund handling = brand damage. Good refund handling = brand asset.

## CHEAT SHEET 18: RED FLAGS — DON'T CLOSE THIS DEAL

If a prospect:

🚩 Demands custom NDA before any sales call
🚩 Asks "is this a scam?" multiple times
🚩 Wants 6-month free trial
🚩 Won't pay any deposit
🚩 Has 1-star review patterns suggesting they're a difficult customer
🚩 Asks you to "guarantee" specific revenue numbers in writing
🚩 Has had 3+ agencies in past year
🚩 Won't get on a video call

→ POLITELY DECLINE. "Doesn't seem like a fit. Best of luck." Walk.

Some clients aren't worth $750/mo. The energy drain costs more than the cash earns.
