# GOATED — DELUSIONAL.md

**Owner:** Tarek
**Updated:** 2026-04-25
**Use:** Wild ideas that could break the market or make a shitload of money. Not all will work. Some will. The point of this doc is to keep the swing-for-fences thinking alive while the day-to-day is grinding cold dials.

---

## How to read this

Each idea has:
- **What it is** (1-3 lines)
- **Why it could win**
- **Why it might fail**
- **Capital to test** (rough)
- **Conviction** (1-10)
- **Time horizon** (when to consider)

Sorted roughly by conviction × ambition. The top ones are the most realistic. The bottom ones are the most delusional. Read top-to-bottom on a Saturday morning when you want to dream big.

**Note:** none of these replace the Mon-Fri grind. Bootstrap first. Build cash flow first. Then start placing bets from this list.

## TIER 1 — High conviction, near-term swings (Phase 0-2 compatible)

### 1. Vertical-Specific Sub-Brands (HVAC.GOATED, Dental.GOATED, etc.)
**What:** Spin up vertical-specific micro-brands and websites. `hvac.goated.solutions`, `dental.goated.solutions`, etc. Each is a vertical-tailored landing page with vertical-specific case studies, voice samples, pricing.

**Why it could win:** SMB owners search vertical-specific. "AI for HVAC" outperforms "AI for small business." Each subdomain ranks differently in Google. Cumulative SEO surface = 10x.

**Why it might fail:** maintenance overhead. 6 micro-sites = 6 brands to keep updated.

**Capital:** $0 — you have the React app, just spin up subdomains.

**Conviction:** 9/10

**Horizon:** Phase 1 (after 5+ closes)

**Implementation:** add subdomain routing to GOATED app. 6 vertical templates. Done in a weekend.

### 2. The Free Audit Twitter Bomb
**What:** Drop public audits of famous brands' websites on X with the GOATED watermark. "Here's $40K/mo Domino's is leaving on the table." Tag the brand. Make it viral.

**Why it could win:** free distribution. Each viral audit = 50K+ impressions. 0.1% click rate on a 50K post = 50 leads. Cost: 1 hour of your time per audit.

**Capital:** $0.

**Conviction:** 9/10

**Horizon:** Now (do one this weekend).

## Implementation philosophy

**You will NOT do all of these.** Most are bad. The point is:

1. Always have 3-5 alternative paths in your head
2. When the main path stalls, you have 30 ideas to pivot to
3. Some of these will become real businesses someone else builds — and you'll know how to evaluate them
4. The exercise of writing this doc trains your strategic muscle

---

## Anti-list — bad ideas to NEVER pursue

❌ "Crypto for SMBs" without a real reason
❌ "AI for healthcare" without HIPAA expertise
❌ "Build the next Salesforce"
❌ "Replace LinkedIn"
❌ Anything requiring 10+ engineers before revenue
❌ Advertising-supported anything (margins are bad)
❌ Free SaaS layer (free users = expensive support)
