**Owner:** Tarek
**Updated:** 2026-04-25
**Use:** Pre-built scripts for every outreach + onboarding moment. Copy → personalize 1-2 fields → send.

---

## How to use

These templates work because they're SHORT, SPECIFIC, and SET UP A NEXT ACTION. Don't add fluff. Don't write "I hope this email finds you well."

For each template:
1. Copy from this doc
2. Replace `{first_name}`, `{business_name}`, `{vertical}`, `{number}` with real values
3. Send within 60 seconds

Templates that take >60 seconds to personalize aren't templates — they're rewrites.

## Loom scripts (90-second audit format)

### 20. Cold-prospect audit Loom (sent post-voicemail)

```
[OPEN] Hey {first_name}, Tarek with GOATED Solutions. (Camera shows your face
or business background, then switches to their website)

[ON SCREEN] Just want to walk through 3 things I'd fix on your site this week.

e.g., "hasn't been updated since November, missing 3 of your services"]. That
costs you about [specific dollar amount] a month in missed local search traffic.

8 PM, [their phone goes to voicemail / their site doesn't have a chat / etc].

walk-through if you want to dig in. Number's in the SMS I sent. Otherwise no
worries — best of luck with {business_name}.
```

**Total: 75-90 seconds.**

## Custom emoji usage

For SMB owners (your ICP), keep emoji minimal:

✅ Use sparingly:
- 🐐 (brand identity, sign-off only)
- 📞 (calendar/calls)
- 📅 (booking)
- 💰 (revenue)
- ✅ (action items)

❌ Avoid:
- 🚀 (cringe to SMB owners)
- 💯 (Gen Z, your ICP is 35-65)
- 🔥 (overdone)
- 🌟 (over-eager)

When in doubt, no emoji. Plain text reads more pro to SMB owners over 35.

## Naming convention for variables

Always lowercase, snake_case, surrounded by braces:

✅ `{first_name}` not `{First Name}`
✅ `{business_name}` not `{businessName}`
✅ `{cal_url}` not `{CalLink}`

This way you can grep + replace consistently.

## When to deviate from templates

You SHOULD personalize beyond find/replace when:
- You found a SPECIFIC finding from their site that no template predicts (mention it)
- Their vertical has a quirk (HVAC seasonality, dental insurance, etc.)
- They mentioned something on the call you can callback ("by the way, how's the new technician hire going?")

You should NOT deviate when:
- You're tired and tempted to write a "really thoughtful" email at 11 PM
- You're trying to be friendly to overcome a no
- You're padding length to seem like effort

**Brevity wins. Personalization wins. Length kills.**
