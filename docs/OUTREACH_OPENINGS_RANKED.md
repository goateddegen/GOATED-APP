# GOATED — Outreach Openings, Ranked

**Owner:** Tarek
**Updated:** 2026-04-25
**Use:** Pick the right opening for the right vertical and energy level. Rotate weekly to prevent script-fatigue.

---

## Why this exists

The opening is 80% of the outcome. The first 7 seconds determine whether they hang up or stay on. This doc gives you **10 ranked openings** + **per-vertical variants** + **5 attention-grabbers beyond lost-rev** so you have ammunition for any prospect, any mood, any week.

**Rule:** memorize TWO openings cold for Week 1. Add a third in Week 2. Don't try to wing all 10 at once.

---

## The 10 openings, ranked by Week-1 conviction

### #1 LOST-REV (start here)
**Score:** 9.5 / 10 — highest signal-to-noise, works for cash-strapped owners.

> "Hey [first name], this is Tarek with GOATED Solutions out of Texas. I'll be quick — I ran your website through our system this morning and we found about $9,000-$12,000 a month in calls and bookings you're losing. Got 30 seconds and I'll show you the three biggest leaks?"

**Why it works:** specific number, specific time horizon, low ask (30 sec), demonstrates effort.
**Why it fails:** sounds salesy if rushed; can backfire if the owner is in a rare "everything's fine" mood.

---

### #2 NEIGHBOR-NAME (best for hot zip codes)
**Score:** 9.0 / 10 — uses local social proof.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek here. I'm working with three other HVAC shops in the Dallas-Fort Worth metro right now and one of them — won't say who — just added 22 booked calls last month. I noticed your shop and figured I'd see if you wanted in before I expand to your zip. Quick question — what's your call-to-booking rate right now?"

**Why it works:** competitor anxiety + curiosity + local relevance. "What's your call-to-booking rate" forces engagement.
**Why it fails:** if you don't have local clients yet, this is risky. Use only after first close in their metro.

---

### #3 PATTERN INTERRUPT (for tired prospects)
**Score:** 8.5 / 10 — best when you're calling someone who fields 20 spam calls/day.

> "Hey [first name] — yes, this is a cold call. I know you weren't expecting this. Want to give me 17 seconds to tell you why I'm calling, and then you can hang up?"

**Why it works:** disarms the auto-reject. Honesty is a rare pattern interrupt.
**Why it fails:** doesn't give them a reason to care. Must follow with a strong hook in those 17 sec.

**Follow-up after they grant the 17 sec:**
> "I run an outbound program for [vertical] shops in your metro. We help shops that miss 30%+ of inbound calls reclaim them with AI agents that book the appointment automatically. Specifically, you guys — your site has [specific tell]. Want to see what we'd do?"

---

### #4 CURIOSITY GAP (for owner-operators with ego)
**Score:** 8.0 / 10 — works on owners who think they know their numbers.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek — quick one. I've been auditing dental practices in Phoenix this week and I noticed something on your site that I'd bet your office manager doesn't know about. Want me to walk you through it?"

**Why it works:** ego hook ("I bet you don't know"), local relevance, low-friction ask.
**Why it fails:** if they push back ("just tell me"), you must have ammo immediately.

**Have ready:** the specific finding from your pre-call audit. Use it within 10 sec of them granting.

---

### #5 SOCIAL PROOF + STORY (best for second close in a vertical)
**Score:** 8.0 / 10 — only works after you've closed 1+ in their vertical.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek with GOATED. Reason for the call — last week I worked with [vertical-similar shop name] in [nearby metro] and we added 14 booked appointments in the first 30 days. I'm reaching out to a few [vertical] shops in [their metro] that fit the same pattern. Got 60 seconds?"

**Why it works:** specific result, specific local context, "few shops" creates exclusivity.
**Why it fails:** without a real reference, this is a lie and they'll smell it. Save for after first close.

---

### #6 STATUS / EXCLUSIVITY (for higher-end prospects, $5M+ revenue)
**Score:** 7.5 / 10 — flips the script. You're choosing them.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek with GOATED. We work with a small list of [vertical] shops we hand-pick for our growth program — we only take three new clients a month per metro. Saw your shop, thought you might fit. Got 90 seconds and I'll tell you what we'd do?"

**Why it works:** exclusivity, qualifier framing (you're vetting them), removes salesy energy.
**Why it fails:** for sub-$1M revenue shops, this is overkill. Use it for med spas, surgery centers, multi-location dentists.

---

### #7 STRAIGHT-SHOOTER (for rural / no-bullshit operators)
**Score:** 7.5 / 10 — Texas-style, works in HVAC / plumbing / roofing.

> "[First name], Tarek here. I'll be straight — I want to sell you something. I help [vertical] shops in [metro] book more jobs using AI. I think I can save you about $X a month in missed calls. Got 60 seconds for me to make the case, or want to call BS and hang up?"

**Why it works:** matches the prospect's communication style. Texas/Southern/Midwest owners RESPECT straight sales.
**Why it fails:** wrong metro. Don't try this on West Coast prospects.

---

### #8 QUESTION-FIRST (low-energy, high-curiosity)
**Score:** 7.0 / 10 — when you're not feeling assertive but still want to dial.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek with GOATED. Quick question — when someone calls your shop after hours, what happens?"
>
> [They answer]
>
> "Got it. Reason I'm asking — we just helped a [vertical] shop in your area capture 18 of those after-hours calls last month. Want to see how?"

**Why it works:** lets them talk first, reduces resistance, the question itself is qualifying.
**Why it fails:** weak closers can lose control of the conversation. Have the pivot ready.

---

### #9 REFERRAL FAKE-OUT (use sparingly)
**Score:** 6.5 / 10 — sketchy unless real.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek with GOATED. Reaching out because we're working with shops referred to us by [common-vertical software brand they likely use, e.g., ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro]. Got 60 seconds to talk about how we plug into [that software] for outbound?"

**Why it works:** if you've actually integrated with their software stack, the implied "referral" is fair game.
**Why it fails:** if you can't back it up under questioning, you've burned the call.

**Only use if you've genuinely built the integration.** Otherwise it's manipulation.

---

### #10 PURE COLD VALUE-DROP (long shot, big reward)
**Score:** 6.0 / 10 — when nothing else has worked that day.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek with GOATED. Don't try to schedule anything yet — I just want to send you a 90-second screen recording of three things I'd fix on your site that would book more jobs this month. No pitch, no calendar invite. Cool to send to this number?"

**Why it works:** zero ask. Builds reciprocity. Best for a hard-no day where you just want to seed Week 2 inbound.
**Why it fails:** doesn't create urgency. Use as last-ditch Week 1 closer, primary Week 3+ tactic once your audit funnel is loaded.

## Per-vertical opening playbook

### HVAC (heating, AC, refrigeration)

**Best opener:** LOST-REV with seasonal hook.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek here. AC season is 90 days out and I just ran your website through our audit — you're losing about $11,000 a month in summer when calls 3X. Got 30 seconds to walk through what we'd fix before May?"

**Hooks beyond lost rev:**
- **Seasonality urgency:** "Summer's coming. You can't add more techs in 30 days, but you CAN stop losing the calls you already get."
- **Tech shortage angle:** "Every HVAC shop is short-handed. AI lets you do more without hiring."
- **Competitor encroachment:** "[Big franchise like One Hour] is moving into your zip."

---

### Dental

**Best opener:** CURIOSITY GAP.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek — I've been looking at your practice site this morning and I noticed something your front desk probably doesn't know about. It's costing you about 6-8 new patient appointments a month. Got a minute?"

**Hooks beyond lost rev:**
- **No-show recovery:** "Your no-show rate is probably 12-18%. We can drop it to 4% with AI confirmations."
- **Insurance verification time:** "Your front desk spends 40% of their day on insurance verification. We automate it."
- **Hygiene recall:** "How many patients are overdue for a cleaning right now?"

---

### Plumbing

**Best opener:** STRAIGHT-SHOOTER.

> "[First name], Tarek — I'll be straight. I help plumbing shops capture emergency calls they're missing because they're on a job. Got 60 seconds?"

**Hooks beyond lost rev:**
- **Emergency-call capture:** "When you're in someone's basement, who's answering the phone?"
- **Maintenance-plan upsells:** "AI can call your customer base every 6 months and pitch maintenance plans."
- **Service area mapping:** "Are you turning down jobs because you don't know what's profitable in your zip?"

---

### Med spa / cosmetic

**Best opener:** STATUS / EXCLUSIVITY.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek with GOATED. We work with a curated list of high-end med spas — only three new clients per metro per month. I think you'd fit our program. Got 90 seconds and I'll show you what we do?"

**Hooks beyond lost rev:**
- **Client lifetime value:** "Your average client is worth $4K+. One missed lead = one missed $4K relationship."
- **Brand image:** "Your competition is using AI. The shops that don't will look outdated by Q4."
- **Membership conversion:** "AI can convert one-time clients to membership in 5x more cases."

---

### Roofing / Solar

**Best opener:** PATTERN INTERRUPT (because they get hammered with calls).

> "Hey [first name] — yes, cold call. I know your phone is ringing all day with people trying to sell you leads. Want to give me 17 seconds to tell you why this one's different?"

**Hooks beyond lost rev:**
- **Storm-chaser fatigue:** "Tired of cold-leads from chase companies? We help YOU generate the leads, not buy them."
- **Inspector booking:** "Our AI books your free roof inspections automatically — even at 11 PM."
- **Insurance claim helper:** "AI helps your customers navigate insurance claims = higher close rate."

---

### Chiropractic / Wellness

**Best opener:** QUESTION-FIRST.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek — quick question. How many patients fall off your schedule between visits 4 and 8?"

**Hooks beyond lost rev:**
- **Retention:** "Most chiro practices lose 40% of patients between visit 4 and 8. AI brings them back."
- **Cash-pay membership:** "Convert insurance-only patients to cash-pay membership. AI handles the pitch."
- **Recovery tracking:** "AI follows up on adjustments — patients feel cared for, retention spikes."

## 5 attention-grabbers BEYOND "lost revenue"

Lost-rev is your default. But variety prevents script-fatigue and lets you re-attempt no's with a fresh angle.

### A. The Specific Tell
**What:** name something on their website / Google profile / reviews that ONLY someone who actually looked could know.

> "I noticed your last 3 Google reviews all mention long phone hold times…"
> "Your booking page has 4 form fields — that's killing 40% of submissions…"
> "Your business hours on Google say closed Sundays but your Yelp says open. Which is right?"

**Why it works:** proves effort, demonstrates competence, creates implicit obligation to listen.

---

### B. The Industry Stat Drop
**What:** quote a recent industry stat that's unsettling, then offer the fix.

> "Did you see the report from [industry rag] saying 73% of [vertical] shops will lose market share to AI-enabled competitors by 2027?"
> "[Industry] customers expect a callback in 4 minutes now — not 4 hours. Most shops are at 4 hours."

**Why it works:** positions you as informed; creates fear; plays to FOMO.

**Make sure the stat is real.** Don't fabricate. There's enough real data; cite it.

---

### C. The Time Anchor
**What:** name a specific time horizon that makes "now" feel urgent.

> "If you started with us in 30 days, you'd see your first 10 booked appointments by July 1."
> "By end of summer, you'd have built a database of 800 reactivatable customers."
> "Q4 of this year is when most shops in your space will start using AI. Right now, you're early."

**Why it works:** specifics create vividness. Vividness creates desire.

---

### D. The Risk-Reversal
**What:** lead with the guarantee, not the price.

> "Hey [first name] — Tarek. Quick one. We have a 30-day money-back deal where if we don't book you 5+ qualified appointments in the first month, you get every dollar back. I think you'd qualify. Got a minute?"

**Why it works:** removes "what if it doesn't work" objection BEFORE it's raised.
**When to use:** prospects who've been burned by other agencies.

---

### E. The Permission Reverse
**What:** instead of asking permission, OFFER to leave.

> "Hey [first name], Tarek. I'll be respectful — if this is a bad time, I'll hang up. If you have 30 seconds, I have something specific for you. Which is it?"

**Why it works:** Gives them control = they don't reject. They're 5x more likely to grant 30 sec when offered the door.

## Opening A/B test framework

You should rotate openings systematically. Track:

| Opening | Dials placed | Connects | Engaged past 30 sec | Bookings | Booking % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LOST-REV | 100 | 8 | 5 | 2 | 25% |
| NEIGHBOR | 80 | 7 | 4 | 1 | 14% |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

After 2 weeks, the data tells you the right opener for YOUR voice + YOUR vertical mix. Don't trust gut — trust the spreadsheet.

---

## When to switch openings mid-week

**Switch if:**
- 50+ dials with the same opening and <2 bookings → script needs work
- You're saying it on autopilot and it sounds dead → freshness drop, rotate
- You closed with a different opening → swap your warm-up routine to that one

**Don't switch if:**
- You had one bad day with the opener that worked last week
- One person told you it sounded scripted (n=1 isn't data)
- You're tired (the issue is energy, not the script)

---

## The "opening graveyard" — DO NOT use these

These look clever but kill conversion:

❌ "How are you today?" → wastes the only 7 seconds you have
❌ "Did I catch you at a bad time?" → trains them to say "yes, I'm busy"
❌ "I'll be brief" → meaningless filler, signals you're not actually brief
❌ "I know you're busy but…" → reminds them they're busy
❌ "I work with companies like yours…" → vague, no specificity
❌ "I'm not selling anything" → you obviously are; lying breaks trust
❌ "Can I have 5 minutes?" → too big an ask. Always 30-60 sec.
❌ "Just checking in" → meaningless, signals no purpose

## Voice / tone notes

The opening's WORDS matter less than how you say them.

**Speed:** medium-slow for the first sentence, normal for the rest. Slow openings = confidence.

**Pitch:** drop your voice slightly at the end of statements (Texas-style). Don't upspeak.

**Pause:** after "got 30 seconds?" — DO NOT FILL THE SILENCE. 3-second silence forces them to say yes or no. Most operators panic and re-talk over their own ask. Don't.

**Energy:** match their energy +1 notch. If they answer flat, be slightly more upbeat. If they answer hyper, slow down.
