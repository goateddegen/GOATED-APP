## Why now

The $1.4 trillion US local-services economy (HVAC, dental, plumbing, med spa, roofing, etc.) is being reshaped by AI:

- **62% of inbound calls to small service businesses are missed** (BIA/Kelsey)
- **AI voice agents now reach human-parity** on intake calls (sub-300ms latency, natural prosody)
- **SMB owners are AI-curious but tool-paralyzed** — they don't have the time or skill to deploy
- **PE rollups in HVAC alone exceed $30B in deal volume since 2022** — proving local services are venture-worthy

The category is: **AI-deployed-as-a-service for the local economy.** This is what GOATED is building.

## The problem (in plain English)

A solo HVAC operator answers 22 of his 35 daily calls. He loses 13. Each lost call is a $400-1500 missed job. Annualized: **$1.5M-5M in lost revenue per year per shop.**

He won't hire an answering service ($800/mo, low quality). He won't build AI himself (no time, no skill). He won't buy enterprise AI (Salesforce sells him on a $50K/yr platform that doesn't fit a 5-person shop).

He needs a partner who:
- Deploys AI for him
- Charges what he can afford
- Delivers in 14 days
- Speaks his language

That's GOATED.

Every other AI agency leads with "we're an AI agency." We lead with **"you're losing $X/month."** Owners don't care about AI. They care about money. Our entire pitch is built around their lost revenue, not our tech.

Most AI agencies promise the moon and ship 6 months later. We deliver Tier 1 in 14 days, working. Owners see ROI before the next bill.

### 5. Founder-market fit
Tarek is 20, autistic, hyper-focused, born to build this. He has a unique advantage: he can dial 100 prospects/day for 12 hours without breaking concentration. That's the operational moat at the founder stage.

## Risks

We name them honestly:

### 1. AI commoditization
Voice AI is racing toward zero-cost. Mitigation: our moat is the playbook + delivery + retention, not the underlying voice tech.

### 2. Big platform competition
ServiceTitan or HubSpot could ship a "GOATED-killer" feature. Mitigation: we're vertical-focused and faster than enterprise platforms; by the time they ship, we'll be deeply embedded with 1,000+ SMBs.

### 3. Founder-only at the start
Tarek is the entire sales team. Mitigation: Phase 1 hires VA + commission closer to defuse this. Documented SOPs by Phase 2.

### 4. SMB churn
Local services have higher churn than enterprise SaaS. Mitigation: 30-day refund forces value delivery; renewal is gated on results, not contracts.

### 5. Regulatory (telecom + healthcare)
TCPA, A2P 10DLC, HIPAA. Mitigation: we built compliant from day 1 — Twilio HIPAA-eligible, full BAA capability for healthcare, A2P 10DLC registered.

### 6. Founder burnout
20-yr-old solo dialing 100x/day. Mitigation: hire VA early (Phase 1), enforce health rituals, sleep mandate, gym 3×/week.
