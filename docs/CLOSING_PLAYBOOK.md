## The one-line mental model

**Solve a measurable problem with a refundable price in a single conversation.**

That's the entire game. Everything below is mechanics on top of that line.

---

## 1. The opening (memorize, don't read)

> *"Hey [First Name], this is Tarek from GOATED. I'm not pitching — I literally just ran a free audit on [their website] and there's something specific I want you to see. Got 30 seconds?"*

### Why this works

- **"I'm not pitching"** disarms the defensive wall every business owner has up
- **"Just ran a free audit"** signals you did real work; you're not a generic spam call
- **"Something specific"** triggers curiosity loop — they want to know what
- **"30 seconds"** is the smallest possible commitment; almost everyone says yes

### Voice mechanics

- **Pace**: 1.0× normal (most cold callers go 1.4×, sounds like spam — slow down)
- **Tone**: peer-to-peer. NOT "May I have a moment of your time, sir." YES "Hey, what's up."
- **Smile**: literally smile while saying the opening. They hear it. It changes everything.
- **Pause** after "30 seconds?" Do NOT keep talking. Wait for their yes/no.

## 2. The number drop (after they say yes)

> *"You're missing about [X] calls a week. Each one is roughly [$Y based on their job size]. That's [$Z]/month walking out the door. I built a system that auto-calls every missed lead within 60 seconds and books them. Want me to send you the audit and we hop on a 15-minute walkthrough this week?"*

### How to size [Y] for any vertical

| Vertical | Avg job/case value | Avg missed calls/week | Headline number/month |
|---|---|---|---|
| HVAC residential | $400-2,500 | 12-20 | $4,800-50K |
| Plumbing | $250-1,800 | 10-18 | $2,500-32K |
| Roofing | $5,000-25K | 4-8 | $20K-200K |
| Med spa | $300-2,000 | 15-30 | $4,500-60K |
| Dental | $400-3,000 | 10-20 | $4,000-60K |
| Chiropractic | $80-300 (per visit, recurring) | 8-15 | $640-4,500 first month, more compounding |
| Auto body | $1,500-8,000 | 5-10 | $7,500-80K |

**Rule of thumb:** if the audit number is < $5K/month, don't pitch the $2,000 setup — the ROI math is too thin and they'll bail. Push them to a free Loom + email follow-up instead, or just dump.

## 3. Common objections — exact responses

### "How much?"
> *"Setup is two thousand. Monthly is seven-fifty after that. Thirty-day refund if I don't recover at least the setup back in month one."*

Then **stop talking.** Silence is leverage. Let them respond.

### "I have a marketing person already"
> *"Cool — show them the audit anyway. If they're already covering this, no harm. If they're missing it, you just got a free second opinion. What's the email?"*

Pivot to email capture. Even if they're not buying today, the audit-shared email is gold for follow-up.

### "I need to think about it"
> *"Of course. The audit's free either way — let me email it. Worst case you have a useful document. Email?"*

Same pivot. NEVER push past one "I need to think." It backfires every time.

### "We tried [Hibu / Yelp / a local agency]"
> *"Right, that's why this is different. Most agencies do generic ads. I'm running missed-call recovery — targeting people who already wanted to call you. Different mechanism, completely. Want to see the audit?"*

Anchor on the difference. Don't talk smack about competitors.

### "Send me an email"
> *"Will do. What's the email and your name spelled out? I'll send within 5 minutes — be looking for it."*

Confirm the email by spelling it back. Send the audit + Loom within 5 min while you're still top-of-mind.

### "Is this AI? I don't trust AI."
> *"It uses AI for the actual call answering. But it's me running the system — you'll be talking to me, not a bot. The bot just makes sure no lead falls through."*

Reframe: AI = tool, you = trusted human.

### "Can you guarantee results?"
> *"I can guarantee the system works. I can't guarantee how many of YOUR missed calls turn into jobs — that depends on your close rate. But: 30-day money back on the setup if it doesn't recover at least the setup amount in tracked revenue. That's me putting my money where my mouth is."*

Specific guarantee > vague promise.

### "Call me back next week / next month"
> *"Sure — but the audit shows you're losing $[X] every week we wait. What's a 15-minute slot Friday at 2 PM look like?"*

Quantify the cost of delay. Always offer a specific time, not "let me know when's good."

### "I'm not interested"
> *"Totally fair. One last thing — can I email the audit anyway? Your call to use it or not."*

If they say no again, "Got it, have a great day" and HANG UP. Never push past two no's.

### "Who is this? How did you get my number?"
> *"Public business listing — I pulled it off Google Maps when I was researching businesses in [their city]. I'm Tarek from GOATED, I run revenue audits. Want me to send you the one I just ran on you?"*

Be transparent. They appreciate it.

### "Are you a real company?"
> *"Yep — goated.solutions. I built it. I'll send you the audit, you can see for yourself. Email?"*

Don't get defensive. Just send proof.

## 4. The booked call — what to do on the discovery call

### Setup (first 60 seconds)
- Confirm you have 15 minutes
- Tell them you'll share screen and walk through the audit live
- "Ground rules: I'm going to be direct, you can interrupt me anytime, and at the end I'll either send you a Stripe link or we'll part ways. Sound good?"

### Audit walkthrough (4 minutes)
- Share screen → open `goated-app-nine.vercel.app/#/for/{their-slug}`
- "Here's what the AI found on your site this morning."
- Walk through: leak headline → top 3 issues → annual revenue impact → AI-recommended fix
- Pause after each. "Does this sound right? Anything I'm missing?"

### Diagnosis question (2 minutes)
- "Putting numbers aside — what's the actual problem you've been trying to solve?"
- LISTEN. Take notes. They will tell you exactly what to sell them.

### Pitch the offer (3 minutes)
- "Here's what I'd run for you — based on what you just told me."
- Outline:
  - Missed-call recovery (AI agent answers in 60 sec)
  - SMS auto-text-back
  - Calendar booking integration
  - Monthly recovered-revenue report
- "$2,000 to set it up, $750 a month after, 30-day money-back."
- Pause.

### Handle their objection (2 minutes)
- They WILL push back on something. Use the response map above.
- If price: anchor the recovered revenue ("audit said $8K/month — this pays for itself in week 2")
- If timing: name the slot ("I have one client opening for [their city] this month")

### Ask for the close (60 seconds)
- "Sounds like a fit. Want me to send the Stripe link now?"
- If yes: paste link, watch them pay (Stripe pings your phone)
- If "let me think": "Cool. I'll text you the proposal + audit. If I don't hear by Friday I'll assume you're a no, no hard feelings."

### Confirm (60 seconds)
- "Welcome to GOATED. I'll text you onboarding details in 60 minutes. Kickoff call Wednesday 10 AM CST work?"
- Hang up. Send the onboarding text immediately.

## 5. Voicemail script (always leave one — boosts callback 3×)

> *"Hey [First Name], Tarek from GOATED. Quick voicemail — I ran a free audit on [their site] and you're missing about [$X] a month. I'll text you the link right now. Thirty seconds and you'll see exactly where. No pitch, just the number. [Your callback number, slowly, twice]."*

Then **immediately** (within 60 seconds) text:
> *"Hey [First Name], Tarek from earlier. Audit: [shareable URL]. Worth a 15-min chat when you've got a sec?"*

The double-tap (voicemail + SMS) is the hack. Most cold callers do one or the other. You do both.

## 6. The mental game

### Before the dial block
- 5 deep breaths
- 1 minute of "I get to do this" reframe (instead of "I have to do this")
- Stand up. Walk around. Don't dial seated and slumped.

### During the dial block
- After every NO: 10 seconds of resetting
- After every YES: 30 seconds of celebration → ramps up energy for next dial
- After 10 dials with zero connects: stand, stretch, water, 1 min outside

### After a brutal call
- Write down what happened
- One question: "What would I do differently?"
- One answer. Move on. Next dial.

### Mid-day collapse (it WILL happen around dial 30)
- This is normal. Every closer hits a wall.
- 5 minutes outside. No phone.
- One push-up set (any number)
- Back at it. Volume always wins.

## 8. Non-lost-revenue hooks (when you don't have audit numbers handy)

When you can't lead with the audit number, use one of these. Ranked by what works for SMB cold:

**1. The peer-reference grab (BEST, when true)**
> "I just signed up [their direct competitor] in [city] last week. Same exact playbook will work for you, want to see what we're doing for them?"
- Triggers: territorial fear, social proof
- Use only when true. Don't lie about competitors — small-town SMB owners talk.

**2. The "I'm picking N clients in your city" scarcity**
> "I'm taking 3 [vertical] clients in [city] this month. You're the second on my list. 30 sec?"
- Triggers: scarcity + flattery
- Solo operator scarcity is genuinely real — you literally can't deliver to 50 clients alone.

**3. The "storm season / busy season" timing**
> "Storm season just hit / It's wedding season / Tax season / Holiday rush — your phone's about to get hammered. Are you set up to catch all of it?"
- Triggers: timely urgency
- Always know what season is coming for the vertical you're calling.

**4. The "I'll do something for free if you don't book" reverse**
> "Hey, even if we don't end up working together, the audit will tell you exactly where you're leaking. I send it for free. Want me to send it?"
- Triggers: reciprocity
- Audit-as-gift, no commitment. Plants a seed.

**5. The "your reviews told me to call" hook**
> "Hey, I was reading your Google reviews — saw 4 different people in the last 6 months mention they couldn't get through on the phone. I fix that exact problem. Worth a sec?"
- Triggers: specific, undeniable, makes them feel seen
- Read their reviews before calling. This is gold.

**6. The "competitor moved in" alarm**
> "Hey [Name], I noticed [Big Brand] just opened a location in [their city]. They have AI booking systems already. You probably need parity. 30 sec?"
- Triggers: competitive threat
- Don't use unless verifiable.

**7. The "I helped someone like you in 7 days" mini-case**
> "Hey, I helped a [vertical] owner in [city] add 11 booked appointments in their first week. That's roughly $[X]. Same fit for you. Worth 30 sec?"
- Triggers: rapid-result proof
- Wait until you have one real case before using.

**8. The pure pattern-interrupt**
> "Hey [Name], this might sound nuts but I built an AI agent that calls back people who hung up on you. Want to see how it works?"
- Triggers: WTF curiosity
- Use sparingly. Works on bored prospects who'll engage with anything weird.

## 9. Per-vertical objection map (handle by industry)

Same objection lands differently in each vertical. Here's how to handle by industry.

### "Too expensive" / "We can't afford it"

**HVAC reply:**
> "One emergency call you catch tonight pays for the setup twice. The math works in week one or you get a refund."

**Med spa reply:**
> "One Botox client you book after-hours = the setup paid back. Two = monthly retainer for two years."

**Dental reply:**
> "One implant case you book — about $4,500 — pays for the entire year of this. Risk on me, not you."

**Roofing reply:**
> "One roof you don't lose — that's a $15K job. The setup is $2K. Math is offensive in your favor."

**SaaS / online reply:**
> "If we add 1 customer/mo at $1K MRR, you're at $12K ARR added. Your CAC has to be under $10K for this not to work, and based on what you said earlier it's probably $4K."

### "I need to think about it"

**Universal reply (works in any vertical):**
> "Of course. While you think — let me email you the audit and a Loom showing exactly what we'd do. That way you have the info. What's the email?"
*(Don't push past one "I need to think." Audit-share is the silver medal.)*

### "I have a person doing this already"

**HVAC / trades reply:**
> "Cool. Run the audit anyway and show it to them — if they're already on it, awesome. If not, you've got receipts. What's the email?"

**Online / SaaS reply:**
> "Sure — I'd compete on this benchmark: [show metric]. If they're hitting that, no need. If not, worth comparing."

### "We tried [Hibu / Yelp Ads / a local agency / ServiceTitan marketing] and it didn't work"

**Universal:**
> "Right, those are paid-traffic plays. I'm not running ads — I'm fixing the leads you already get that fall through. Different mechanism. Want to see the audit?"

### "Send me an email"

**Universal close-it-down move:**
> "Will do — I'll send within 5 minutes. While I have you: what's the actual problem you're trying to solve right now? I want to make sure the email matches what you care about, not generic info."
*(Get them to tell you their pain BEFORE the email — your follow-up converts 3× higher.)*

### "I don't have time right now"

**Universal:**
> "Totally fair. When's better today — 2 PM or 4 PM, your call?"
*(Force a specific time. "Anytime later" = never.)*

### "Are you AI or a real person?"

**HVAC reply:**
> "I'm Tarek, real person, 20 years old, building this thing solo. The product uses AI for the actual phone-answering. You'll always talk to me, not a bot."

### "Is this scam?"

**Universal — answer with confidence + receipts:**
> "Fair to ask. goated.solutions — I built it. I'll send you the audit so you can see for yourself. If it's not legit, the audit will be useless. Email?"

### "Just give me the price"

**HVAC / brick-and-mortar:**
> "Two thousand setup, seven-fifty a month, 30-day refund. Want me to send the link?"
*(Give the price, then immediately ask for the close. They were likely about to buy.)*

**Online / Tier 2:**
> "Range is $10-20K setup + $2.5-5K/mo depending on scope. Discovery call to scope it. 15 min Friday?"

### "What's your guarantee?"

**Universal:**
> "30-day money back on the setup if I don't recover at least the setup amount in tracked revenue. So worst case: you lose 30 days of waiting, no money."

### "I want a contract"

**Tier 1:**
> "I'll send a one-page agreement with the Stripe link. Standard terms — 3-month minimum on monthly, refundable setup, mutual 30-day cancel after that. Sound fair?"

**Tier 2:**
> "Yes — proper SOW + MSA goes out after the discovery call. Want to book the call now?"

### "Can you put it in writing what you'll do?"

**Universal:**
> "Yes — after you sign up I send a Notion doc with everything we'll build, week-by-week. Want me to send the link first or do you have other questions?"

### "What if it doesn't work?"

**Universal:**
> "Setup refund kicks in. We part ways, no hard feelings. The risk is on me — I only make money if this works."

## 10. Attention-grab beyond "lost revenue" (8 angles ranked)

Lost revenue is the default because it's quantifiable. But these can land harder in specific situations:

| # | Angle | When to use | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lost revenue (your default) | First 50 dials | None — works everywhere |
| 2 | Peer/competitor reference | After you close 1 in their city | Only if true |
| 3 | Pattern interrupt ("most direct call") | Day's been slow, energy reset | Some prospects find it gimmicky |
| 4 | Curiosity hook ("noticed something specific") | High-quality prospect, want to qualify-up | Vague — can backfire |
| 5 | Insider/list-builder | Med spas, dentists, prestige verticals | Don't oversell scarcity |
| 6 | Reverse free-give ("I send the audit either way") | Stubborn prospect, won't engage | None |
| 7 | Reviews-told-me-to-call | When their reviews mention phone issues | Requires research |
| 8 | Storm/season urgency | Roofing, HVAC, retail, tax prep | Must match real season |

**The meta-rule:** rotate 2-3 openings per dial block. After 30 dials, if conversion is low, try a different angle. Don't do 100 dials with the same opening — diminishing returns.

## 8. Red flags — disqualify fast, save your time

### Don't pitch them if:
- Less than $50K/year revenue (can't afford even Tier 1)
- They mention "we don't take new patients/clients" (they're full)
- They've been in business < 6 months (too volatile)
- They want to "white-label" your service (broker — bad margin)
- They keep saying "send me everything in writing first" (won't ever close)
- They want to negotiate setup below $1,500 (won't see value)

### Pitch them HARD if:
- Multiple Google reviews mentioning "didn't return my call" or "no answer"
- Owner picks up the phone themselves (decision-maker access)
- They're hiring (sign of growth budget)
- Their website has a phone number prominently (phone-led business)
- They mention they tried Yelp Ads, ServiceTitan, or another lead service (they have a budget)

## 9. Logging every call

In your Google Sheet CRM, after EVERY dial, log:
- Time
- Outcome (5 categories): No answer | VM left | Talked-no interest | Talked-interested-no book | Booked
- 1-line note: "Owner answered, said too busy, told me to call Tuesday"

Why? Three reasons:
1. **Patterns**: after 100 dials you'll see what time/vertical/script line works best
2. **Follow-up**: "I called Monday, you said try Tuesday — it's me again"
3. **Sanity check**: when you feel like nothing's working, the log shows: 87 dials → 6 connects → 1 book. That's normal. Keep going.

## 10. The closer's creed

Read this before every dial block:

```
The phone doesn't know my reputation.
The phone doesn't know who said no last time.
The phone doesn't know I'm broke.
The phone only knows if I'm on it.

I am the most prepared person on this call.
I have run their audit. I know their numbers.
I know what they're losing every week we don't work together.
I am offering a real solution at a fair price with a refund clause.

If they say no, that's information, not failure.
If they say yes, I'll deliver the result I promised.
If they ghost, that's about them, not about me.

Next dial.
```

## 11. Things that DO NOT work (skip these)

- ❌ Reading from a script word-for-word (sounds robotic)
- ❌ Asking "How are you doing today?" (trigger phrase for spam-detection brain)
- ❌ "I'd love to help you" (filler, weak)
- ❌ "Quick question" (also flagged as spam pattern)
- ❌ Long opening monologue before pausing (lose them in 10 seconds)
- ❌ Mentioning AI in the opening (raises suspicion before trust is built)
- ❌ Sending a 12-page PDF proposal (nobody reads them)
- ❌ "Do you have time for a 30-minute call?" (no one does — ask for 15)

## 12. After the close — 48-hour onboarding sprint

### Hour 0 (still on the call)
- Stripe Payment Link sent
- Confirmation: "Welcome aboard. I'll text you onboarding details within an hour."

### Hour 1
- Send onboarding email:
  - Welcome line
  - What's happening this week (kickoff Wed, system live Mon)
  - 1-2 things you need from them (Twilio number access, calendar, intake form)
  - Direct phone number (Google Voice for the metro)

### Day 2 — Kickoff call
- 30-min Zoom
- Walk through the system you're building
- Get access to: Twilio/OpenPhone, calendar (Cal.com or Calendly), CRM if any
- Set expectation: "Live Monday, first report Friday"

### Day 5-7 — Go live
- Test missed-call → AI voice → SMS → calendar flow
- Send a "we're live" text to the client

### Day 14 — First check-in
- "Here's how week one looked"
- Ask: any objections from staff? Any tweaks?

### Day 30 — Renewal moment
- Show recovered revenue report
- "Want me to keep going for another 30?" — they say yes (because the math works)
