## PHASE 0: BOOTSTRAP ($0 → $10K MRR)

**Where you are: now.**

### Goals
- First close in Week 1
- 5 closed clients by end of Month 1
- 10 active clients ($10K MRR) by end of Month 3

### Daily rhythm
- 75-100 dials/day, M-F
- 1-3 sales calls held/day
- 1 close per 100 dials (initially), trending to 1 per 50 by Month 3

### Tech stack at this phase
- React + Vercel (already deployed)
- VAPI for voice agents
- Twilio for SMS + numbers
- Stripe for payments
- Supabase for auth + DB
- Cal.com for booking
- Google Sheets for CRM (yes, sheets — keep it simple)

**Total tech cost at 5 clients:** ~$300-500/mo.
**Net margin at 5 clients × $750/mo:** ~$3,250/mo.

### Financial discipline
- Run lean. NO Notion Plus, NO Slack paid, NO fancy SaaS until you must.
- Reinvest 50% of every closed deal into ops (Twilio, VAPI usage, lead-list buys).
- Personal salary = $2K/mo. The rest stays in the war chest.
- Open a separate bank account for GOATED — never co-mingle.

### Key metrics to track daily
- Dials placed
- Connect rate
- Booking rate
- Close rate
- Cash collected
- MRR

**One spreadsheet, updated nightly. Compare week-over-week.**

### Biggest risks Phase 0
1. **Burnout from solo dialing.** Mitigation: walk every 90 min, sleep 8 hrs, gym 3×/wk
2. **Bad client (refund threat).** Mitigation: clearly document scope, deliver in writing, proactive weekly checkins
3. **Cash crunch.** Mitigation: front-load closes early in month, defer ALL non-essential spend
4. **Imposter syndrome.** Mitigation: keep a wins log; reread Friday. Volume is your shield.

### Phase 0 graduation criteria
**Move to Phase 1 when:**
- $10K MRR sustained for 60 days
- Week-over-week dial cadence stable (you're not burning out)
- 80%+ of clients renewing past initial 3 months
- Wait list of inbound interest forming

## PHASE 1: FIRST HIRE ($10K → $30K MRR)

### Goals
- 30 active clients ($30K MRR) by end of Month 12
- Free Tarek's time from low-leverage work
- Build first repeatable systems

### First hire: Virtual Assistant (VA)
**Role:** List building, audit pre-runs, CRM upkeep, basic SMS follow-ups
**Where to hire:** OnlineJobs.ph or Latam (Pampa, GoRemotely)
**Rate:** $5-8/hr × 30 hrs/wk = $600-960/mo
**ROI:** frees Tarek 15 hrs/week to dial more

**First week of VA:**
1. Set up shared Notion/Google Drive
2. Train on 1 prospect-list build (record Loom, walk through)
3. Train on the GOATED audit page workflow
4. Train on CRM update protocol
5. Daily 15-min standup at 8 AM CST

### Second hire: Commission-only closer
**Hire when:** $20K MRR, can't dial enough
**Compensation:** 30% of first-month revenue + 10% of recurring for 6 months
**Where to find:**
- Cole Gordon's community / Belkins / Sales-Ghyver
- Twitter "AI agency closer" search
- Referrals from your existing clients (some have sales-y friends)

**Risk:** wrong closer kills deals. Vet via:
- 30-min mock pitch on a fake prospect
- Pay $500 deposit-test: pay them upfront for 1 week of dials, see if they book
- Call references

### Tech upgrades at Phase 1
- Move CRM from Sheets to **HubSpot Free** ($0) or **Pipedrive** ($14/mo)
- Add **Zapier** ($30/mo) for cross-tool automation
- **Loom paid** ($15/mo) for personalized video pitches
- **Slack** ($0) for VA + closer communication

**Total tech cost at 25 clients:** ~$1,500-2,000/mo.

### Financial discipline at Phase 1
- Keep personal salary at $5K/mo MAX. The rest stays for hires + ops.
- Hire #2 (closer) AFTER hire #1 (VA). VA pays for closer's tools.
- Maintain 90-day cash runway in business account at all times.

### Key metrics to add at Phase 1
- Cost per acquired client (CAC)
- Lifetime value (LTV) — currently estimating
- Churn rate (monthly %)
- Customer satisfaction (NPS quarterly)

### Biggest risks Phase 1
1. **Hiring wrong people.** Mitigation: 30-day probation, clear deliverables
2. **Quality drop as you scale.** Mitigation: SOPs documented for everything
3. **Tarek refuses to delegate.** Mitigation: track your time. If you're doing <$50/hr work, fire yourself from it.

### Phase 1 graduation criteria
**Move to Phase 2 when:**
- $30K MRR for 60 days
- VA + closer both delivering value (you're net-positive after their cost)
- Documented 5+ SOPs that anyone could execute
- Churn under 5%/mo

## PHASE 2: SYSTEMS ($30K → $100K MRR)

### Goals
- 100 active clients ($100K MRR) by Month 18-24
- Productize delivery — anyone on the team can onboard a new client
- Hire ops to free closers from non-selling work

### Hires this phase (in order)

1. **Operations Manager** ($60-80K/yr) — runs delivery, manages VAs
2. **Second Closer** (commission, similar terms)
3. **Onboarding Specialist** ($45K/yr) — runs Day 1-30 with new clients
4. **Junior Developer** ($60-80K/yr or contractor $50-75/hr) — builds custom modules
5. **Content Marketer** ($50-70K/yr or contractor) — content that generates inbound

**At end of Phase 2:** 8-10 person team.

### Tech investments
- Move from Stripe Payment Links to **Stripe Subscriptions** with proper billing
- Implement **HubSpot Pro** ($800/mo) for full CRM + marketing
- Build **internal admin dashboard** in your existing GOATED app (you already have the foundation)
- **Slite or Notion** for SOP repository ($10-50/mo)
- **PostHog** for analytics ($20-100/mo)

**Tech cost at 100 clients:** ~$8-15K/mo.
**Net margin at 100 × $1,500/mo (avg with upsells):** ~$130K/mo.

### Productize delivery
This is the most important phase task.

**Build:**
- 14-day onboarding checklist (every client gets the same)
- Pre-built VAPI templates per vertical (HVAC, dental, plumbing, etc.)
- Standard SMS sequences per use case
- Standard reporting (weekly email, monthly dashboard)
- Automated billing + invoicing
- Self-service docs site

**Goal:** any team member can onboard a new client in 14 days WITHOUT Tarek.

### Channel diversification
Phase 0-1 is 100% outbound. Phase 2 add:
- **Inbound from content** — Tarek (or content marketer) ships 2 case studies + 1 LinkedIn post / week
- **Referral program** — 1 month free for any client who refers a closed client
- **Partnerships** — integrate with 1-2 vertical-specific software (e.g., Housecall Pro for HVAC)

### Financial discipline at Phase 2
- Tarek personal salary: $10-15K/mo
- Maintain 6-month cash runway
- 30% of revenue → reinvested into team + tech
- 10% of revenue → set aside for taxes (you're going to owe)
- 60% of revenue → margin

**Hire a fractional CFO** or accountant. You're now a real business.

### Key metrics at Phase 2
- ARR (annualized recurring revenue)
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) — target 110%+
- Gross margin per client
- Sales-rep productivity (closes per closer per month)

### Biggest risks Phase 2
1. **Founder bottleneck.** You're still the salesperson, the closer, the technical lead. If you don't delegate by month 18, you'll plateau.
2. **Bad ops manager.** Wrong hire here cascades. Spend 60+ days hiring right.
3. **Burning out the team.** Make sure VAs and closers see the upside.
4. **Tech debt.** GOATED is a fast-moving codebase. Hire a junior dev to maintain it.

### Phase 2 graduation criteria
**Move to Phase 3 when:**
- $100K MRR sustained for 90 days
- Team of 8-10
- Tarek does <40 hrs/week on direct sales
- Inbound > 30% of new clients
- Documented playbook anyone could execute

## PHASE 3: SaaS SPIN ($100K → $300K MRR)

### The fork in the road

Phase 3 is where you make the SaaS bet. **You don't have to.** Some agencies happily run at $100K-300K MRR forever as services businesses. But the GOATED tech is built for SaaS — you'd be leaving leverage on the table not to spin it up.

### Goals
- Launch self-serve SaaS layer
- 50% of new revenue from SaaS by end of Phase 3
- 200-300 SaaS users + 100 active agency clients

### What gets productized (the SaaS spin)

The agency taught you what SMBs ACTUALLY need. Now build it as a self-serve product.

**Tier 1 SaaS: GOATED Lite ($297/mo)**
- AI voice agent for missed calls (templated, 80% of agency value, 20% of agency price)
- SMS auto-text-back
- Basic GMB optimization
- Customer-facing dashboard
- Self-serve onboarding (no human touch)

**Tier 2 SaaS: GOATED Pro ($797/mo)**
- Everything in Lite
- Custom voice prompts
- Review automation
- Reactivation campaigns
- Slack/email support

**Tier 3: GOATED Enterprise (custom, $2-10K/mo)**
- Everything in Pro
- White-label option
- Multi-location
- Custom integrations
- Dedicated success manager

### Hires this phase

1. **Head of Product** — own the SaaS roadmap
2. **2-3 Senior Engineers** — scale the platform
3. **Designer** — make the product feel premium
4. **Customer Success Manager (SaaS-specific)** — onboard self-serve users
5. **Demand Gen Marketer** — paid ads + SEO + content

**End of Phase 3:** 15-20 team.

### Channel changes
Phase 3 marketing motion:
- **Content/SEO** is now primary inbound (not Tarek's LinkedIn)
- **Paid ads** on Meta + Google for SMB owners
- **Vertical-specific landing pages** (hvac.goated.solutions, dental.goated.solutions, etc.)
- **Affiliate / referral program** at scale (10% recurring lifetime)
- **Outbound continues** for higher-tier deals only ($5K+ deposits)

### Financial considerations Phase 3
- Optionally raise capital (see INVESTOR_PITCH.md)
- OR continue bootstrapping (your cash flow supports it)
- Either way: you can pay yourself $25-40K/mo

**The raise question:**
- **Don't raise** if you can hit $300K MRR profitably. SaaS bootstrapped > venture-funded.
- **Raise $1-2M** only if you see a 3-year window to dominate a vertical that someone else might capture
- Don't raise more than you need

### Tech investments
- Move infrastructure from Vercel to dedicated cloud (AWS or GCP) for cost at scale
- Implement proper observability (Datadog, Sentry, PostHog)
- Build internal automation tools (deploy automation, customer onboarding flows)
- Security: SOC 2 Type 2 (required for enterprise customers)

### Phase 3 graduation criteria
**Move to Phase 4 when:**
- $300K MRR
- SaaS = 30%+ of revenue
- Net Revenue Retention 120%+
- Team of 15-20

## PHASE 4: CHANNEL + GEOGRAPHIC ($300K → $1M MRR)

### Goals
- $1M MRR ($12M ARR) by Month 60
- Multi-vertical, multi-geographic dominance
- Partnership-led growth

### Strategic moves

**1. White-label offering**
Other agencies can resell GOATED under their brand. They take 30%, you take 70%, no client-facing work for you.

Implementation:
- Build white-label option in product
- Onboard 10-20 partner agencies
- Each partner brings 5-20 clients on average
- 100-400 partner-driven clients = $300-800K MRR add

**2. Geographic expansion**
- Open Mexico City + Latin America office (low-cost talent, growing SMB market)
- Open London office (UK SMB market is similar to US)
- Partnership with regional resellers in EU + AU

**3. Vertical playbooks**
By Phase 4 you should have 6-12 vertical-specific playbooks:
- HVAC, dental, plumbing, med spa, roofing, chiropractic + extensions

Each playbook = pre-built voice prompts, integrations, content, SOPs. Sells faster than horizontal.

**4. M&A**
Acquire small competitors:
- 2-3 person agencies in HVAC vertical (consolidation play)
- Voice AI startups that haven't hit traction (talent + tech acqui-hire)
- Lead-gen companies with prospect databases

### Hires this phase
- VP Sales
- VP Engineering
- VP Marketing
- VP People (HR)
- Heads of regional offices
- ~25-50 total team

### Financial structure
- Optionally Series A ($5-15M) for M&A + geo expansion
- OR keep bootstrapping at this margin
- Personal: $50-100K/mo or take a meaningful liquidity event (founder secondary)

### Phase 4 graduation criteria
**$1M MRR achieved when:**
- 12-month revenue CAGR 100%+
- 80%+ gross margins
- Sub-3% monthly churn
- 1,000+ active customers across SaaS + agency

## CRITICAL PRINCIPLES (apply at every phase)

### 1. Cash flow > vanity metrics
Every phase: focus on cash IN, not headlines, not press, not Twitter followers. Cash compounds. Vanity doesn't.

### 2. Solo founder discipline forever
Even at $1M MRR, Tarek does 1 cold call per week to stay calibrated. The day you stop selling is the day the business gets soft.

### 3. Hire slow, fire fast
One bad hire at any phase costs 3-6 months. Always 3-interview minimum. Always 30-day probation. Always reference checks.

### 4. Build the thing, then write about it
Don't pre-announce features. Don't tease. Build → ship → write a case study → repeat.

### 5. Optimize for optionality
At each phase, you should have 3 paths forward (more bootstrap, raise, sell). Don't lock yourself in.

### 6. Pay yourself what's needed, not what's wanted
Higher salary later. War chest now. Cash compounds.

### 7. Document everything
Every SOP, every script, every call recording. Your future hires need this. Your future SaaS needs this.

### 8. Stay in the dial chair
The biggest mistake founders make at $50K MRR: they stop selling. Keep dialing. Forever. It calibrates everything.

## Scenarios you might face

### Scenario A: Phase 1 is harder than expected
**Trigger:** $10K MRR for 6+ months, can't break through.
**Likely cause:** Bad lists, weak script, or wrong vertical.
**Fix:** Pick ONE vertical, go deep. Don't generalize. Become the HVAC AI agency. Or the dental AI agency. Specialization unsticks plateaus.

### Scenario B: Phase 2 you're crushing it (faster than plan)
**Trigger:** $50K MRR by month 6.
**Likely cause:** You hit a vertical-product-channel fit early.
**Action:** DON'T accelerate hiring beyond plan. Cash flow > headcount. Hire ops first, NOT more closers.

### Scenario C: Phase 3 SaaS doesn't take off
**Trigger:** Self-serve conversion <2%, churn >10%.
**Likely cause:** Product UX bad, onboarding broken, target user doesn't self-serve.
**Action:** Stay agency-led. There's no shame in $300K MRR services business. SaaS is optional.

### Scenario D: Phase 4 you can't scale beyond $500K MRR
**Trigger:** Stuck at half a million for 12+ months.
**Likely cause:** Hit ceiling of single-vertical, single-geo TAM.
**Action:** Multi-vertical OR international OR partnership-led OR acquire complementary business.

## When to consider selling the business

**Year 3:** strategic acquirers (Salesforce, ServiceTitan, HubSpot) might offer 5-10x ARR. Consider if:
- Tired
- Want optionality
- Have a higher-leverage idea

**Year 5:** PE acquirers offer 4-6x ARR for cash flow.

**Year 7-10:** IPO possible if growth sustains.

**Don't sell early.** Phase 0-2 valuations are bad. You're trading sweat equity for cash discount. Run to Phase 3+ minimum.
