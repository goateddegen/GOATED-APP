**Owner:** Tarek
**Updated:** 2026-04-25
**Use:** The complete financial playbook from $0 to $1M MRR. Cash management, runway, reinvestment triggers, hiring math, profit allocation. Read before each phase transition.

---

## North-star financial principles

1. **Cash compounds. Vanity doesn't.** Every dollar reinvested at the right phase = $5-10 in revenue 12-18 months later. Every dollar spent on lifestyle = $0 future.
2. **Stay solvent forever.** Never go below 90 days of expenses cash on hand. Even if you have to slow growth.
3. **Pay yourself enough to survive, not enough to celebrate.** Founder salary creep is the #1 killer of bootstrapped agencies.
4. **Two accounts always.** Personal + Business. Never co-mingle. Even at $0 MRR.
5. **Treat the IRS as a silent partner.** They get 25-30% of everything. Set it aside the day you collect.
6. **Reinvest from gross, save from net.** Reinvestment comes BEFORE owner pay. Salary is a residual.

## Part 1 — The cash flow equation

```
CASH IN (revenue)
  − Cost of delivery (Twilio, VAPI, Stripe fees, contractor labor)
  = GROSS PROFIT
  − Tax reserve (25-30%)
  = POST-TAX PROFIT
  − Reinvestment (tools, hires, growth)
  = OWNER DRAW (your salary)
  − Personal expenses
  = SAVINGS / WAR CHEST
```

Memorize this. Every dollar enters at the top, settles at the bottom. Most founders skip the tax reserve and the reinvestment line. They go bankrupt.

## Part 2 — Phase-by-phase financial playbook

### Phase 0 ($0 → $10K MRR) — bootstrap survival

**Revenue assumption:** 5-10 active Tier-1 clients × $750/mo = $3.75K-$7.5K MRR + $2K-$5K/mo in setup fees.

**Burn (your costs):**
- Vercel: $0 (Hobby plan covers it)
- Twilio: $50-150/mo (numbers + calls + SMS)
- VAPI: $50-200/mo (depending on call volume)
- Anthropic API: $20-50/mo
- Stripe fees: 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction = ~3% of revenue
- Domain + email: $30/mo
- Misc tools: $50-100/mo

**Total burn at 5 clients:** ~$300/mo. **At 10 clients:** ~$500/mo.

**Allocation:**
| Bucket | % | $ at $10K MRR |
|---|---|---|
| Cost of delivery | 5% | $500 |
| Tax reserve (Personal + Business) | 25% | $2,500 |
| Reinvestment (lead lists, tools, savings) | 50% | $5,000 |
| Owner draw | 20% | $2,000 |

**Owner salary cap: $2,000-3,000/mo at this phase.** Live lean. Live nomadic. The war chest is your job.

**Cash flow rules:**
- Charge setup fees UPFRONT (before delivery). Lock in cash.
- Run on Net 0 (paid before service starts) for monthly retainers.
- Stripe payment links (already setup) — money lands in 2 days.
- Open business bank: **Mercury** or **Brex** (free, online, fast).

**Trigger to move to Phase 1:**
- $10K MRR sustained 60 days
- $30K cash in business account
- Tarek can spend 10+ hrs/week on something OTHER than dialing

### Phase 1 ($10K → $30K MRR) — first hires

**Revenue assumption:** 30 mixed clients (~25 Tier-1 + 5 Tier-2 starter deals) = $30K MRR + $5K-15K/mo setup fees.

**Burn:**
- Tools (same scale): $1,000-2,000/mo
- Stripe fees: ~3% of revenue
- VA (offshore): $600-1,000/mo
- Closer commission: 30% of first-month + 10% trail = scaling cost
- Misc: $500/mo

**Total burn at 30 clients:** ~$5,000-8,000/mo (variable).

**Allocation:**
| Bucket | % | $ at $30K MRR |
|---|---|---|
| Cost of delivery (incl. closer commissions) | 20-25% | $7,500 |
| Tax reserve | 25% | $7,500 |
| Reinvestment | 30% | $9,000 |
| Owner draw | 20-25% | $6,000-7,500 |

**Owner salary: $5,000-7,500/mo.**

**Hiring math (don't hire too early):**

| Role | When to hire | Cost | When they pay back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Virtual Assistant | $10K MRR | $600-1K/mo | Within 30 days (frees 15 hr/wk dialing) |
| Commission Closer | $20K MRR | 30% of first-mo + 10% trail | Within 30 days (each close > their cost) |
| Onboarding Specialist | $40K MRR | $3-4K/mo | Within 60 days (handles 10+ new clients) |
| Operations Manager | $60K MRR | $5-7K/mo | Within 90 days (frees Tarek 20 hr/wk) |
| Junior Dev | $80K MRR | $4-7K/mo | Within 120 days (custom builds, upsells) |

**Key principle:** every hire should pay back in 90 days OR you don't hire them.

### Phase 2 ($30K → $100K MRR) — systems

**Revenue assumption:** 100 mixed clients ($75K from 75 Tier-1 + $25K from 5 Tier-2 + $30K/mo setup fees) = $100K MRR + $30K/mo setups.

**Burn:**
- Team (5-8 people): $25-40K/mo
- Tools at scale: $5-10K/mo
- Stripe fees: ~3%
- Marketing experiments: $2-5K/mo
- Office (optional, virtual mailbox + occasional coworking): $500/mo

**Total burn: ~$35-55K/mo.**

**Allocation:**
| Bucket | % | $ at $100K MRR |
|---|---|---|
| Cost of delivery + team | 35-45% | $40,000 |
| Tax reserve | 25% | $25,000 |
| Reinvestment | 15% | $15,000 |
| Owner draw | 15-25% | $20,000 |

**Owner salary: $15,000-20,000/mo.** Lifestyle goes up — nice Airbnbs, business class, occasional splurge. Still no car, no real estate.

### Phase 3 ($100K → $300K MRR) — SaaS spin

This is the fork. Either bootstrap OR raise.

**Bootstrap math:** at $100K MRR / 90% gross margin / 30% net margin → **$30K/mo to reinvest in SaaS spin-up.** Slow but steady. 18-24 months to spin up properly.

**Raise math:** $1-2M seed buys you 18-month runway to spin SaaS faster + hire ahead of revenue. You give up 15-25% equity but compress timeline by 12 months.

**My recommendation:** bootstrap unless you find the perfect strategic backer. Money is cheaper later. Equity now = expensive forever.

### Phase 4 ($300K → $1M MRR) — channel + geo

At this scale you have actual decisions to make:

- **Hire CFO** (fractional, $5-10K/mo). Stop being your own accountant.
- **Convert from cash basis to accrual** accounting (proper books).
- **SOC 2 Type 2** ($30-50K) — required for enterprise SaaS clients.
- **Tax structure optimization** — possibly C-corp conversion if SaaS dominates revenue.
- **Personal tax-deferred maximization** — Solo 401(k) up to $70K/yr pretax.

## Part 4 — The reinvestment decision tree

When you have $X in extra cash this month, where does it go?

```
Is this the first $5K/mo of profit?
  → Build emergency fund first (6 mo of personal expenses in HYSA)

Have you maxed your Roth IRA this year?
  → Yes → continue down. No → contribute to Roth ($7K/yr, top priority)

Do you have 90 days of business burn in the bank?
  → Yes → continue down. No → top up business reserve.

Is there a hire that would pay back in 90 days?
  → Yes → hire them.
  → No → continue down.

Is there a tool/system that would 10x output (e.g., better CRM, ad budget that's been validated)?
  → Yes → invest. No → continue down.

Is there a mature SaaS idea ready to spin up?
  → Yes → fund it. No → continue down.

→ Save remaining 50% in war chest, deploy 30% to taxable brokerage, 20% to lifestyle/family.
```

## Part 5 — When to lend, never to lend

### Cash flow advances (avoid)
- Stripe Capital — only AFTER 6+ months of revenue history
- Brex / Mercury credit lines — useful for 30-day float, NOT for funding hires
- AmEx Plum / Brex business credit cards — 60-day float, pay in full

### When NOT to take debt
- ❌ To fund a hire who hasn't proven yet
- ❌ To fund marketing experiments
- ❌ To "smooth" lifestyle
- ❌ To buy assets (cars, equipment)
- ❌ Personal credit lines for business

### When debt makes sense
- ✅ Bridge between booked revenue and Stripe payout (rare, 3-day float)
- ✅ Buying inventory for fulfilled-order revenue (not your model)
- ✅ Acquiring a smaller competitor at 1.5x EBITDA when you have cash flow to support
- ✅ Equipment financing on assets that immediately produce revenue

## Part 6 — Tax strategy (don't skip this)

### Quarterly estimated taxes

You'll owe quarterly to the IRS once you're profitable.

| Quarter | Due |
|---|---|
| Q1 | April 15 |
| Q2 | June 15 |
| Q3 | September 15 |
| Q4 | January 15 (next year) |

**Set aside 25-30% of profit monthly.** Pay quarterly. Don't get surprised April 15.

### Texas advantage

Texas has NO state income tax. If you're domiciled in Texas, you keep more. Things to maintain:
- TX driver's license
- TX voter registration
- TX address (real or virtual mailbox is OK)
- Avoid spending >183 days in any single OTHER state
- Document your travels (some states audit nomads)

### LLC tax structure (defaults are usually wrong)

Single-member LLC default = "disregarded entity" → pass-through to your personal taxes. Simple. **Wrong** above $50K profit.

**Better at $50K+ profit:** elect S-Corp tax treatment.
- Pay yourself a "reasonable salary" (W-2 wages)
- Take rest as distributions (no SE tax = saves 15.3%)
- Net savings: $5-15K/yr at $50-100K profit

**Hire a CPA** the moment you see $50K profit. ~$500-2,000/yr fee. Saves 10x in tax planning.

### Deductions you'll commonly miss

- Home office (% of rent, utilities)
- Phone bill (% used for business)
- Internet (same)
- Travel for business (flights, lodging, 50% meals)
- Client meals (50%)
- Equipment (laptop, monitor, headset)
- Software subscriptions (all of them)
- Professional development (courses, books, conferences)
- Health insurance premiums (if self-employed)
- Solo 401(k) contributions (up to $70K/yr)

Track everything in **QuickBooks Self-Employed** ($25/mo) or just **Notion** + monthly export.

## Part 7 — Insurance you actually need

| Insurance | Phase | Cost | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Health** (Marketplace ACA) | Always | $300-700/mo | Don't go uninsured. Ever. |
| **Liability (general business)** | Phase 1+ | $500-1,500/yr | Protects against client lawsuits |
| **Errors & Omissions (E&O)** | Phase 1+ | $1-3K/yr | Specifically for service-business mistakes |
| **Cyber insurance** | Phase 2+ (when handling client data heavily) | $1-3K/yr | Required for enterprise contracts |
| **Term life** | When you have dependents | $30/mo | $500K-1M policy if you have a partner |
| **Disability** | Phase 1+ | $50-100/mo | If you can't work, biz collapses |

**Don't buy:**
- ❌ Whole life insurance (scam)
- ❌ Annuities (scam)
- ❌ Identity theft monitoring (DeleteMe + Privacy.com cover the practical risk)

## Part 8 — Personal financial discipline

### The first $100K saved

This is the hardest. After $100K, compounding does the work. Get there fast.

**Math:** if you save $5K/mo from $20K-30K/mo MRR for 24 months → $120K saved + ~$10K interest = **$130K personal liquid by Phase 2.**

### Rules for the first $100K

- No financed purchases over $1K (cars, watches, etc.)
- No subscriptions you don't use weekly
- No splurge that exceeds 1% of personal liquid (e.g., $1K splurge needs $100K liquid)
- Always negotiate (subscriptions, rent, services — 80% of asks succeed)
- Cash for under-$100 purchases (mental friction = less spending)
- 30-day rule on >$500 non-essentials (wait 30 days, see if you still want it)

### Splurge budget

- 5% of personal allocation → splurge
- Earn it: hit MRR target → unlock the splurge
- Categories that compound: travel, health, books, experiences with people you love

### Lifestyle creep prevention

| If your MRR doubles | Your rent should increase by | Your transport should | Your food should |
|---|---|---|---|
| $10K → $20K | 0% | Stay the same | +25% (better quality, not luxury) |
| $20K → $50K | +25% | +25% | +50% |
| $50K → $100K | +50% | +50% (still rentals) | +100% |
| $100K → $300K | +50% | +100% (still rentals) | +200% (chef-prepped if you want) |

The principle: **lifestyle scales by sqrt(MRR), not linearly.**

## Part 9 — When to take chips off the table

**At Phase 2 ($100K MRR sustained 90+ days):**
- Convert 20% of war chest into permanent personal investments
- Diversify: 70% S&P, 20% BTC, 10% cash
- Treat this as "I cannot lose this money"

**At Phase 3 ($300K MRR sustained):**
- Take a "founder secondary" if raising — $100-500K personal liquidity
- Buy a primary residence ONLY if you've lived in one place 12+ months and plan to stay 3+ years
- Otherwise keep renting

**At Phase 4 ($1M MRR):**
- 50% of net new income → diversified holdings
- Set up trusts for tax efficiency
- Consider citizenship-by-investment (St. Kitts, Portugal D7) for optionality

## Part 10 — Common founder financial sins (don't commit these)

1. ❌ **Lifestyle inflation matching MRR inflation.** You're at $50K MRR, not $50K take-home.
2. ❌ **Buying a Tesla / luxury car at any phase.** Worst flex at every level.
3. ❌ **Not separating personal + business accounts.** Pierces the LLC veil legally.
4. ❌ **Forgetting taxes are coming.** Set aside 25-30% the day you collect.
5. ❌ **Loaning money to friends/family.** Kills relationships. Gift if you must.
6. ❌ **Buying a house at Phase 0-2.** Illiquidity + commitment = founder kryptonite.
7. ❌ **Starting a separate startup before this one is profitable.** Focus.
8. ❌ **Investing in friends' startups.** You're not an angel investor at $30K MRR.
9. ❌ **Day-trading personal money.** You don't have an edge.
10. ❌ **Co-signing for friends/family.** Your credit, your liability.
11. ❌ **Skipping insurance.** One ER visit = wipeout if uninsured.
12. ❌ **Co-mingling with a partner.** Romantic + financial entanglement = disaster risk.
13. ❌ **Hiring before you have the cash flow.** Hire from cash, not from hope.
14. ❌ **Underpricing to "build a portfolio."** Race-to-bottom; never recover the price up later.
15. ❌ **Refusing to charge upfront.** Your cash flow dies waiting on Net 30.
