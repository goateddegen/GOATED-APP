## Part 1 — LinkedIn

### Decision: GO DARK ON PERSONAL LINKEDIN. Build COMPANY PAGE.

**Personal LinkedIn:**
- DO NOT delete (sudden delete sometimes flags background-check services and looks suspicious)
- Set profile to **private/anonymous mode**:
  - Settings → Visibility → Profile viewing options → "Private mode"
  - Settings → Visibility → Edit your public profile → turn OFF public profile
  - Hide profile from search engines: Settings → Visibility → "Public profile and URL" → Off
- Strip headline/bio of anything searchable (replace with generic "Currently building.")
- Hide all activity, connections, photos, posts
- Keep the account dormant — don't post, don't react, don't accept connections
- Optional: change first name to "Tarek A." (LinkedIn allows initial-only last name)

**Company LinkedIn (GOATED Solutions):**
- Create new Company Page: linkedin.com/company/goated-solutions
- Logo + cover image with brand
- Headline: "AI for Local Service Businesses"
- About: 200-word company description, no founder name
- Industry: "IT Services / Marketing & Advertising"
- Headquarters: "Texas, USA" (no specific city)
- Founded: 2026
- Specialties: "AI voice agents, lead generation, SMB automation, local services"
- 5-10 seed posts: case studies, audits, brand updates
- Post 1-2x/week from this page going forward

**Time to set up:** 30 min total.

## Part 3 — Google & search results cleanup

### Search yourself

Open incognito. Google: `"Tarek Ali"` + variations with your city/college/anything identifying. Note every page-1 result.

For each bad result:

1. **You can request removal:**
   - Google "Outdated Content" tool: removes cached snippets after the original page is gone
   - Google personal-info removal: search.google.com/legal-removal-troubleshooter (works for phone, address, ID #s)
   - Direct outreach to webmaster: ask politely
2. **You cannot remove (e.g., news articles):**
   - SEO suppression — push them down with NEW content
   - Build clean content: GOATED Solutions website, Crunchbase listing, Product Hunt page, Medium articles
   - Each new positive page = one ranking spot up for the new content, one down for the bad

### Data brokers (the big one)

These sites publish your name + address + phone + relatives. Most prospects never check them, but bad-faith trolls do.

**Manual removal (free, takes ~3 hrs):**
- Whitepages
- Spokeo
- BeenVerified
- Intelius
- Radaris
- TruePeopleSearch
- FastPeopleSearch
- PeopleFinder
- USSearch

Each has an opt-out form. Search "[site name] opt out" → fill the form → wait 7-14 days.

**Automated removal ($10-15/mo):**
- DeleteMe (joindeleteme.com) — best one
- Optery
- Privacy Bee

**Recommendation:** start with DeleteMe ($129/yr). It hits 30+ data brokers automatically. Worth every dollar for ongoing maintenance.

## Part 4 — LLC and business identity

### Use a registered agent service

When you file LLC, the registered agent's address goes on public record. Don't use your home address.

**Options ($50-100/yr):**
- Northwest Registered Agent (most private, $125/yr)
- Stable.app (modern, $35/mo includes mail scanning)
- ZenBusiness
- Incfile

This means the LLC's public-record address is the registered agent's, not yours.

### LLC structure

- **Name:** GOATED SOLUTIONS LLC
- **State:** Texas (your state) OR Delaware (better for future fundraising). For now: Texas is simpler.
- **Address on file:** registered agent address
- **Member listed:** "Tarek Ali" (you, legally, but only on internal LLC docs — Texas does NOT publish member names publicly by default)

### Business contact info

| Surface | Use |
|---|---|
| **Business phone** | Twilio number ((555) XXX-XXXX) — NEVER your personal cell |
| **Business email** | tarek@goated.solutions or hello@goated.solutions |

### Domain registration

## Part 5 — What to say to prospects

The "who are you?" question rarely comes up on a first call. When it does:

**Casual ask ("Who are you guys?"):**
> "I'm Tarek, the founder of GOATED Solutions. We're based in Texas. We help local service businesses capture more calls and bookings using AI."

**Skeptical ask ("Are you guys legit?"):**
> "Totally fair. Here's our website [link], here's a case study from a [vertical] shop in [metro]. We're a small Texas-based AI agency — about 30 active clients. If you want, I can put you in touch with a current client as a reference."

**Aggressive ask ("What's your last name? I want to look you up"):**
> "Tarek. The company is GOATED Solutions, all our public-facing info is on goated.solutions — happy to send you our LLC paperwork or a reference. What specifically do you want to verify?"

Don't volunteer last name unsolicited. Don't lie if asked directly. Pivot to company verification (which is the actual thing they care about).

## Part 6 — Sales call hygiene

### Before the call

- Block your caller ID via Twilio settings (display GOATED SOLUTIONS instead of Tarek's name)
- Use Krisp or similar voice modulation if you want extra layer
- Sit in a clean background (no personal photos, no family in frame)

### During the call

- Use first name only when introducing
- Don't mention your city specifically — say "Texas"
- Don't mention your age (especially if you're 20 — it's only an asset on second/third call once trust is built)
- Don't mention school / personal history
- Stay in operator mode: "We do this. We've worked with this. Here's what we'd do for you."

### After the call

- Send follow-up from `@goated.solutions` email, not personal Gmail
- Calendar invites from `@goated.solutions` calendar (Cal.com supports custom domains)
- Loom videos: account name = "GOATED Solutions" not "Tarek Ali"

## Part 9 — Edge cases

### "What if a prospect has me on a list of 'sketchy AI bros'?"

If a specific prospect knows your old reputation: you have two choices.

1. **Address it head-on, briefly:** "Yeah, look, I had a chaotic stretch online when I was younger. I built this agency to put my time and energy into real, measurable work. Happy to show you results, not history."
2. **Let GOATED speak:** "I appreciate the candor. Here's what we've built. Here's a current client who'll vouch. What matters here is whether we can save your shop $X/month — let me show you the audit."

Don't argue. Don't defend. Just redirect to value.

### "What about my professional licenses / past employers / college?"

If they're on your LinkedIn and you go dark, most disappear from public search. If they're on background-check sites, they may persist. **Don't worry about these for SMB sales.** Your prospects don't background-check.

### "What if someone DMs me trying to dox me?"

Don't engage. Block. Move on. Tarek the operator does not exist on social media in 2026. Tarek the founder appears only on contracts and bank statements.

### "What about Glass / Wellfound / OnlyFans / weird old accounts?"

Run a deep search yourself: Google your old usernames, your old emails, common variations. Shut down or scrub anything searchable. Most platforms have account deletion or privacy lockdown.
